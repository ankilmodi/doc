## 🧪 How to Use (on https://momentum-signal-tracker.vercel.app/)

### Step 1: Get Signals
1. Click **"Start Scan"**
2. Wait for signals to appear
3. Signals show: Symbol, LTP, RSI, Momentum, Entry, Targets, Stop Loss

### Step 2: Place Orders
Each signal has a **BUY** or **SELL** button:
- Click the button
- Confirm the order
- Order is placed in **PAPER mode** (safe testing)

### Step 3: View Orders & Positions
Scroll down to see three tabs:
- **Open Orders** - pending orders
- **Order History** - completed orders
- **Positions** - active positions with P&L

### Step 4: Manage Positions
- Click **"Cancel"** to cancel an order
- Click **"Exit"** to close a position
- Auto-refreshes every 10 seconds
